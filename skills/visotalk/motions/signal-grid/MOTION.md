# Signal Grid

A cool paper sheet ruled into twelve numbered columns; every idea is a module set flush under a heavy ink rule, one signal yellow blocks what is being said, and the board re-flows instead of cutting.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not

- A visible grid: twelve hairline columns, a ruler 01–12, a running head and folio.
- Every idea is a module: a heavy ink rule, a mono index and label, content flush left. No boxes, radii or shadows.
- One signal, yellow, always a block behind ink: the word, line, row, bar or step being said, and its columns on the ruler.
- The board never cuts: modules re-flow across columns; a new page arrives by columns.

Closest: Red Pencil (a page marked by hand) and Lab Notebook (drawn on squared paper). Neither sets running text, tables, code, worked equations and charts with axes on one board; this does. Not Stage Light or a slide (centred title, bullets).

## 2. Ground and material

`sheet.tsx`, one global part: cool grey paper, mono head and folio over a 3 px rule, the numbered ruler, twelve columns in 96 px margins, a floor at 872 px. `reveal` fills the columns while modules move; `slot` outlines a landing. White (`panel`) only for a detail module or a flow block.

## 3. Colour roles

`data` (yellow) is a block behind ink, never text, one thing at a time plus its ruler columns; a plotted line that matters gets a yellow band under its ink. `text` ink: type, rules, bars, axes. `muted`: labels, notes, sources, lines not spoken about. `faint`: hairlines, empty slots. Several series: ink, grey, outlined paper. `good` / `bad` only on a state's tag.

## 4. Type and captions

Hanken Grotesk (`sans`, `display`): headlines 72–84, text 38–48, labels 30–40, figures 110–132. JetBrains Mono (`mono`) for head, ruler, module labels, notes, code, 23–30. Chinese: Noto Sans SC. Never forced uppercase. Caption: a square ink bar, a yellow rule on its left.

## 5. Motion

Exact; appear and leave in 0.3 s. A re-flow is 0.9–1.2 s with `reveal` up. The ruler's signal slides in 0.35–0.6 s. A block wipes in 0.5 s on its word. Items set one per spoken item. A page turns in 1 s, slabs 35 ms apart, modules swapped while covered. Lines draw in about 1 s, an axis rescales in 1.3 s; figures count in 1.5–1.8 s.

## 6. Composition and framing

Headline top left; modules from 340 to 872 px, 7 + 5, 6 + 6 or 12 columns. Every module starts on a column line and stays inside the margins at rest. A lesson carries a module into the next page by re-flowing it; the folio counts pages.

## 7. Sound

A paper tick on landing, a slide under a re-flow, a run of ticks under a column turn, a click per count.

## 9. Native moves

- **Block the word.** Yellow wipes behind the phrase as said. *Fits content like:* the word deciding a tense; a root cause.
- **Light the columns.** The ruler's yellow slides to the module said. *Fits content like:* code to its cost; chart to its number.
- **Re-flow, don't cut.** A module narrows; another unrolls into the freed columns. *Fits content like:* a timeline making room for a card.
- **Turn by columns.** Twelve ink slabs drop left to right and lift on the next page. *Fits content like:* a new section; question to answer.
- **Build into ruled slots.** Items set one by one into numbered slots. *Fits content like:* three causes; four years tested.
- **Work it out.** An equation line by line on its equals sign. *Fits content like:* U = IR; an error times the years.
- **Ride and rescale.** A line draws with its value riding the tip; the axis widens to show how far it went. *Fits content like:* 0.9 days becoming 9.8.
- **Count to the figure.** A big figure counts to its exact value. *Fits content like:* 365.2422 days; +18% YoY.

## 10. Pitfalls

- Two yellows, or a ruler lit on all twelve. Fix: the ruler lights the module said.
- Ruler yellow off the column lines. Fix: whole col and span at rest.
- A module in the margin after a slide. Fix: col + span ≤ 13 at rest.
- Chinese clipped in a box sized for Latin. Fix: let text wrap.
- A value rounded: print it as said; count-up keeps decimals.
- A list under the captions. Fix: six items, above 872 px.
- A pie of close shares. Fix: a share bar; a ring for 2–4 parts.

## 11. Freedom in each work

Far apart: a lecture building one argument to a definition; a report from a big figure through chart and table to two risks; a code walkthrough, the listing left while its cost re-flows beside it.
