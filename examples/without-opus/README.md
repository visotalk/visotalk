# Without Opus

The second VisoTalk promo, made with VisoTalk: Mandarin, 16:9, 4:09, in the voice `minimax:Chinese (Mandarin)_Radio_Host` at 1.0×. It asks whether a
cheap model can make an explainer worth sharing. Opus writes four motions down, DeepSeek makes four films with them, and each film is shown beside the
Opus sample it learned from, flaws included. The conclusion: taste can be written down; 2D fully, 2.5D too, and 3D still rests on the model. The
narration, line by line, is [`source/script.md`](source/script.md).

## The evidence

All four films ran once, with `openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017` under our benchmark harness, each brief naming its motion and a question the
motion's sample does not cover. The costs are model tokens only; the voice is extra.

| Motion | Question | Length | Model cost | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Night Diagram (2D) | What happens when a nerve cell fires | 1:05 | $0.089 | 10.5 min |
| Earth Block (2.5D) | What is buried under a street | 0:55 | $0.136 | 15.8 min |
| Plumb Line (2D) | How high the atmosphere goes | 0:59 | $0.139 | 14.3 min |
| Light Bench (3D) | How a lens forms an image | 0:57 | $0.211 | 25.5 min |
| No motion (control) | How a lens forms an image | 0:59 | $0.122 | 17.1 min |

The film's $0.58 is the first four rows. It leaves out a first Night Diagram run that a truncated model reply ended after 28.5 minutes, at $0.113.

What the evidence does not show, and the film should not be taken to claim:

- **One run each**, on questions chosen by the people who made the motions.
- **"3D still rests on the model", and "a motion cannot write down where the camera goes"**, are inferences. No Opus control was run on the lens question.
- **The two no-motion controls disagree.** On the lens question no motion stayed 2D and explained more clearly than Light Bench; on the Roadster question the motion won by a long way. The film says both.
- **"More rounds of fixing will improve it" was not tested.** No film went through a repair round, and the staircase on that page is labelled as an illustration.
- **The 21 motions** count this branch's four, which have to be merged for the number to be true.

## Rebuild

```sh
cp examples/visotalk-intro/audio/music.mp3 examples/without-opus/audio/    # once
pnpm visotalk qa examples/without-opus/doc.tsx
pnpm visotalk build examples/without-opus/doc.tsx --out <dir>
```

A rewritten line is voiced again with `MINIMAX_API_KEY` (a China account also sets `MINIMAX_BASE_URL=https://api.minimaxi.com`). Two things the checks
report are known and left: `qa` finds the wall under the caption and the camera push on the taste page clipped by its frame, both on purpose. `build`
reports some videos as not loaded (`net::ERR_ABORTED`) when it closes the page; each one loaded with no error in a player checked by seeking, though
the cause was not confirmed.
