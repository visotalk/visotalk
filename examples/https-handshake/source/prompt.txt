# Brief T1: what happens when a browser opens an HTTPS site

Make an English explainer video for curious non-engineers: what happens when a browser opens an HTTPS website. Explain the TLS handshake in plain terms (checking the certificate, agreeing on keys, then talking in code) and why someone listening on the same Wi-Fi sees only gibberish. It must run 50 to 70 seconds.

Show on screen the things the narration talks about; do not make it a deck of bullet-point slides. Research, the script, the visuals and the production are otherwise yours to decide. Do not stop to ask anything; deliver the finished page.

## Fixed rules

- **Narration:** MiniMax text-to-speech only, voice `English_Trustworthy_Man`, speed 1.0. The key is in the environment as `MINIMAX_API_KEY`, and the account is a mainland China one, so the API base is `MINIMAX_BASE_URL` (`https://api.minimaxi.com`). No other speech engine, and no narration audio taken from elsewhere.
- **Subtitles:** shown on the page in sync with the narration.
- **Delivery is a web page, not a video file:** `out/index.html` plus any files it needs inside `out/`, referenced by relative paths. A 1920×1080 stage. Opening the page in a browser and clicking play must show the whole video with its narration and subtitles.
- **Never render or encode a video** (MP4 or any other format), and never export frame by frame. Single-frame screenshots to check your own work are fine.
- **Notes:** write `out/NOTES.md` with the script, the sources of any material you used, how to play the page, and anything left unfinished.
